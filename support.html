FORME Support

How can we help?

Find quick answers below. If you still need help, email us and include the email address associated with your FORME account plus a short description of what happened.

Account & sign-in

If you can’t sign in, confirm you’re using the same email address you used to create your FORME account. Password reset and account access questions can be sent to support.

Adding clothing

Use the + button or FORME Quick Actions to add clothing. If your current plan has reached its closet limit, FORME will offer the option to upgrade your closet.

Styling & weather

Weather-aware styling is optional. If live weather is unavailable, FORME can use your saved manual weather preference instead.

Plans & subscriptions

Your plan controls closet capacity and certain usage limits. Subscription billing and cancellation are managed through your Apple ID and the App Store.

Privacy & your closet

Your wardrobe and profile are associated with your authenticated FORME account. For more detail, read our Privacy Policy.

Contact FORME

For support, contact us at formevirtualcloset@gmail.com.
