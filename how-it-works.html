How it works

From your closet to your next look.

Add the pieces you own, tell FORME what you have planned, and use your wardrobe in more intentional ways.

01 · Build your closet

Start with what’s yours.

Add clothing to your virtual closet and keep the pieces you actually wear in one organized place.

02 · Tell FORME the moment

Style for where you’re going.

Describe the occasion in your own words. FORME can build around pieces you choose and, when enabled, consider the forecast too.

03 · Make it yours

Build, rate, refine.

Prefer to create the outfit yourself? Use Make It Yours to assemble a look, then ask FORME to rate it and give practical styling feedback.

04 · Keep what works

Save the looks you want again.

Favourite combinations stay close at hand, so getting dressed can become faster without becoming repetitive.
