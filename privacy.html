Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026. This Privacy Policy explains how FORME Virtual Closet processes information when you use the FORME app and website.

1. Information you provide

When you create or use a FORME account, you may provide information such as your email address, wardrobe photographs and clothing details, saved looks, styling preferences, profile information, and optional body- or fit-related information that you choose to enter.

2. Information used to provide FORME features

FORME processes information needed to provide features such as closet organization, outfit suggestions, saved looks, outfit ratings, account access, synchronization or backup features, and plan entitlements.

If you choose to use weather-aware styling, location or weather-related information may be used to provide recommendations that are more appropriate for current conditions.

3. Account and cloud services

FORME uses third-party service providers to support features such as authentication and cloud functionality. These providers may process information on behalf of FORME only as needed to provide their services. FORME uses Supabase-based services for account authentication and certain cloud functionality.

4. Photos and wardrobe content

Clothing photographs and wardrobe information are used to provide your virtual closet and styling experience. FORME is designed for photographs of clothing and accessories and does not require facial photographs. You should avoid uploading sensitive personal imagery that is not required for wardrobe functionality.

5. Styling information

Information you choose to provide about your style preferences, proportions, dimensions, fit preferences or other styling considerations may be used to personalize outfit recommendations and styling feedback.

6. Purchases and subscriptions

If paid FORME plans are purchased through Apple, payment and billing transactions are processed by Apple. FORME may receive information about your subscription status or entitlement that is necessary to provide plan features. FORME does not receive your full payment card information from Apple.

7. How information is used

  • Provide and maintain FORME features.
  • Authenticate accounts and keep user accounts separate.
  • Organize and display your virtual wardrobe.
  • Generate outfit suggestions and styling feedback.
  • Save looks and preferences associated with your account.
  • Provide optional weather-aware styling.
  • Manage plan access and entitlements.
  • Provide customer support and respond to requests.
  • Maintain the security, reliability and performance of FORME.

8. Your choices and account deletion

You can choose whether to provide optional styling information and whether to use optional weather-aware features.

You can permanently delete your FORME account from within the app. Open Profile, go to the Account section, choose Delete my FORME account, and confirm the deletion. When deletion succeeds, FORME deletes the account and associated account data handled by FORME, subject to limited information that may need to be retained where required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or dispute-resolution purposes.

If you have an active App Store subscription, deleting your FORME account does not automatically cancel that subscription. Subscription changes and cancellation are managed through your Apple Account subscription settings.

Questions about your account, your data, data export, or other privacy requests can be sent to formevirtualcloset@gmail.com.

9. Data retention and security

FORME retains information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain account functionality, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and protect the security of the service. Account data that is eligible for deletion is removed when a valid in-app account deletion request is completed.

Reasonable safeguards are used to protect information. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Third-party services

FORME may rely on service providers such as Supabase and Apple to provide authentication, cloud functionality, purchases, subscriptions, or related platform services. Those services may process information according to their own terms and privacy practices in addition to their role supporting FORME.

11. Children

FORME is not intended for children under the minimum age required to independently consent to digital services in their jurisdiction.

12. Changes to this Privacy Policy

FORME may update this Privacy Policy as the app, website or services evolve. When material changes are made, the effective date shown at the top of this page will be updated.

13. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact FORME at formevirtualcloset@gmail.com.
