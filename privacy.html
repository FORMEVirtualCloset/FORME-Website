Privacy Policy

Effective October 31, 2026. This policy explains the information FORME may process when you use the app and website.

Important: This is a practical launch draft based on FORME’s current product design. It should be reviewed against the final App Store privacy disclosures and production backend configuration before public release.

1. Information you provide

When you create or use a FORME account, you may provide information such as your email address, wardrobe photographs and item details, saved looks, styling preferences, profile information, and optional body or fit-related dimensions that you choose to enter.

2. Information used to provide features

FORME may process the information needed to provide closet organization, outfit generation, saved looks, outfit ratings, account access, backup or synchronization features, and plan entitlements. When you enable weather-aware styling, location or forecast-related information may be used to tailor recommendations.

3. Account and cloud services

FORME uses service providers to support features such as authentication and cloud functionality. These providers process information on FORME’s behalf according to their role in providing the service. FORME currently uses Supabase-based authentication in the app.

4. Photos and wardrobe content

Clothing photos and wardrobe information are used to provide the closet and styling experience. FORME is not designed to require facial photographs, and users should avoid uploading sensitive personal imagery that is not needed for wardrobe functionality.

5. Purchases

If paid plans are offered through Apple, purchase and billing transactions are handled by Apple. FORME may receive information about subscription status or entitlement necessary to unlock plan features, but does not receive your full payment card information from Apple.

6. How information is used

  • Provide and maintain FORME features.
  • Authenticate accounts and preserve account separation.
  • Generate styling recommendations and outfit feedback.
  • Provide customer support and respond to requests.
  • Maintain security, reliability and product performance.

7. Data choices

You can choose whether to provide optional styling information and whether to use weather-aware features. Account deletion, data export or other privacy requests can be sent to our support email.

8. Data retention and security

FORME keeps information for as long as reasonably needed to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes and maintain security. Reasonable safeguards are used, but no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children

FORME is not intended for children under the minimum age required to independently consent to digital services in their jurisdiction.

10. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as FORME evolves. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised when material updates are made.

11. Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to our support email.
